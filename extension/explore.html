Ask the shared graphs anything in SQL (SQLite). Everything runs in this page; nothing is uploaded. Back to the graph

Tables

owners(username, position)
whose graphs are loaded; position 0 is the first
people(username, name, private, verified)
everyone in any loaded graph
follows(source, target)
source follows target
circles(owner, username)
username follows the owner, or the owner follows them
edges(a, b)
view: follows in both directions, for paths

Each crawl only sees follows among its owner's own people. A follow between two people who share no loaded circle is missing from the data, so "none found" there means "can't see".

Usernames in results open that person in the graph.
